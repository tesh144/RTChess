⚡ 13-Step Service Pipeline

1
Batch Customer Entry TAP OUTSIDE
Player taps outside queue. All waiting customers enter and form line at once.
2
Customer Enters Restaurant AUTO
First customer auto-walks through door into restaurant.
3
Waiter Seats Customer TAP CUSTOMER
Player taps customer. Nearest waiter seats them at empty table.
4
Take Order TAP SEATED CUSTOMER
Player taps seated customer. Waiter takes order, order appears above customer.
5
Chef Gets Clean Plate TAP ORDER
Player taps order. Nearest chef walks to plate rack, picks up clean plate (plate-first cooking).
6
Chef Cooks Dish AUTO
Chef auto-walks to cooking station with plate and cooks ordered dish.
7
Chef Delivers to Counter AUTO
Chef manually carries completed dish from cooking station to serving counter (no teleport).
8
Waiter Picks Up Dish TAP DISH
Player taps completed dish on counter. Nearest waiter picks it up.
9
Waiter Serves Customer AUTO
Waiter auto-walks to customer's table and serves dish.
10
Customer Eats & Auto-Pays AUTO
Customer eats dish (timer). Payment happens automatically when done eating. Customer leaves, dirty plate remains on table.
11
Waiter Collects Dirty Plates TAP DIRTY PLATE
Player taps dirty plate. Waiter collects it. Can stack multiple dirty plates by tapping multiple tables before washing.
12
Waiter Takes Plates to Sink AUTO
Waiter auto-walks to washing station with dirty plate stack.
13
Waiter Washes Plates TAP WASHING STATION
Player taps washing station. Waiter washes ONE plate at a time (even if carrying multiple). Clean plate returns to plate rack. Repeat taps for each additional dirty plate.
